1. ABOUT THIS DOCUMENT
1.1This Disclosure Agreement is made between Glomax Solar Enterprises ("Glomax") and the customer named in it (the "Customer") for the solar package under this ticket number.
1.2This document is a reference and agreement between Glomax and the Customer. The printed warranty and contract are handled at the installation, outside the system.

1.4The GLOMAX system only handles the request, the roof assessment and the approval. Payment, installation and warranty are handled by Glomax outside the system.
1.5This agreement covers solar packages only. Materials-only purchases are handled by Glomax Staff outside the system.
2. SCOPE OF WORK
2.1Glomax is responsible for installing the solar package shown in the Disclosure Agreement at the property address in the request. The Partner Installer assigned to the Customer does the installation.

2.3Installation time depends on the package and the property. Hybrid systems and tall houses may take longer.

3.1Prices on the Glomax website are estimates. The final price is the amount in this Disclosure Agreement, confirmed after the roof assessment.
3.2The Customer pays Glomax at the Glomax office by cash, GCash or bank transfer, following the payment terms in this Disclosure Agreement. The GLOMAX system does not take or record payments.
3.3Glomax gives the Customer an original official receipt. The Customer should keep every receipt as proof of payment and of the warranty.
4. WARRANTY
4.1Glomax gives a 1-year warranty on the whole solar system. It covers accidental damage.
4.2The warranty starts after the installation is finished.
4.3The official receipt and the text messages between the Customer and Glomax Staff are the proof of purchase and of the warranty.
4.4For a warranty concern, the Customer shall contact Glomax Staff first. If a service visit is needed, Glomax Staff arranges it with the Partner Installer and confirms the schedule with the Customer.
4.5Damage from natural calamities is not covered by the warranty. For this kind of damage, the Customer pays for the labor, materials and service fee.
